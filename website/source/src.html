The product

For the coach.

A diary that tells people.

Hours, days away and standing lessons. Call a day off for rain, and everyone affected is told.

Capture as it happens.

Photo, video or a voice note, filed under the lesson as you take it.

Draw on the clip.

Pause, draw, talk over it, and the player receives the recording. Or play two clips side by side, in step.

Log a lesson in seconds.

Tap the player. Tap what you worked on. Log it.

For the player.

Every lesson, kept.

Your coach's note and every clip, on the lesson's own page.

Drills with a day.

Set by your coach for the next lesson, with a reminder at the hour you choose.

Progress you can see.

Your level over time, the areas you have worked on, and your attendance.

Study it, side by side.

Compare two clips in step, or go through one frame by frame.

For the family.

Parents, in the loop.

Book for a child, follow their lessons, answer for them.

Under-18s.

Their own account, with their lessons and drills. Booking stays with the family.

A parent's words are the parent's.

In a child's thread, a parent's line carries the parent's name, so it never reads as the child's.

Each family's own thread.

A coach's message to a group arrives in each family's own thread. No family reads another's.

Reminded once.

The evening before each lesson, everyone is told.
