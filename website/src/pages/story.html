The story

From the Irish nascadh, linking.

Nosca is an anglicised spelling of the Irish word nascadh, which means linking.

A coach, a player and a family, linked.

One moment

A coach picks up the phone mid-lesson, does one thing in seconds and puts it down.

Every coach screen is judged against it.
