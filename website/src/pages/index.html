On WhatsApp? Bring it with you

WhatsApp, in as lessons.

A coach’s chat with a player comes in as dated lessons: what was worked on, that day’s clips and photos, and the coach’s own lines, word for word.

How the chat is kept safe.

Read on the phone
WhatsApp’s export is opened and read on the phone itself.
The coach’s words only
Only the coach’s written messages go to Anthropic’s AI to be sorted, a day at a time, with the names Nosca knows, numbers and links taken out first.
No clip, photo or voice note
Videos, photos and voice notes never go to the AI.
Nothing until ticked
Nothing is added until it is ticked, and a day the sorting is unsure of starts unticked.
Held until finished
Nobody else sees the lessons until the import is finished.
Removable
An import can be removed at any time: its lessons at once, its files within the hour.

Log a lesson in seconds.

For the coach: who, what was worked on, Log it. Video, photo and voice go with it, and the register is a tap away.

Every lesson, kept.

For the player: the coach’s clips, photos and notes in one feed. A parent books and answers for a child.
