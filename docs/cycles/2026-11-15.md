## The large item
- **The business card, and the page its second code opens.** `scripts/business-card.py` writes MOO's four files — a colour PDF and a separate black-foil PDF a side, 88 × 59 mm with the 84 × 55 mm trim boxed — and three previews into `docs/brand/business-card/`, whose README is the order (Gold Foil Business Cards on Super 380 gsm with the Soft Touch finish, MOO size, rounded corners, gold on both sides, 50 to start) and the spec check against MOO's rules. The front is the brand's bottle green edge to edge with fine gold pinstripes across the top and bottom thirds and the mark and NOSCA in a clear band — the composition of MOO's own foil sample the founder held, and the lockup of the app's front door; the back is white with the mark in foil, NOSCA in green, Fionn Breathnach · Founder, "One app for coach, player and parent", the six sports, and two codes, Open Nosca and Add to phone, drawn as vector squares and read back from a print-resolution render with OpenCV. The type is Instrument Sans, the face the app names after Switzer. `alt-olive/` is the front on the olive the founder floated, for the hand to decide.
  - **Research that shaped it:** MOO foils only its Super paper — Luxe, the thick uncoated stock, takes no foil — so "matte luxury with gold foil" is Super with Soft Touch, which is the sample card. Foil comes as its own black vector file; lines at least 1 pt, foil type at least 10 pt, foil under 30 % of a side, nothing printed under a foil shape; trim 84 × 55, bleed 88 × 59, safe 80 × 51; printed type 8 pt and up. A QR code on a card wants 2 cm or so, a four-module quiet zone and dark on light: these are 16.5 mm version-3 codes at 0.57 mm a module in the brand green on white, 11:1.
  - **The page:** `/install` — for anyone, signed in or not — is the Home Screen guide: iPhone (an iPad too) · Android · Computer on the app's Segmented, opened on the device it is read on, the steps as rows in the phone's own words with the phone's own glyph, Safari's Share and Chrome's three dots and Samsung's three lines; where Chrome or Edge offers to install, one Install Nosca button opens the browser's own sheet; an iPhone's last step says "sign in there once", because iOS gives a Home Screen app its own storage. The same guide is Settings › Add to Home Screen (gone once the app runs from a Home Screen) and the arrival screen's quiet line after sign-up, which is the founder's "first you do the app, and then afterwards it will do the instructions".
