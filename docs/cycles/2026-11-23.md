# 21–23 November — the white look, the club colours, the website, the cards' address

Three days of the founder steering by voice, one pull request (#156). The words that set each turn are in CLAUDE.md beside what they changed; this is the record of what shipped, what proved it, and what is still owed.

## Shipped
- **21 November — white, Duolingo's keys, Apple's type.** White pages and Apple's greys in every sport, a box as a white card on a grey line with its ledge, keys on ledges, the system face on one ladder, Chat in Messages' shape, less text, Settings in groups. CLAUDE.md: "White, one saturated colour a sport…".
- **22 November — every surface on that look.** Six lenses audited every screen; the verified findings merged in four batches and two rounds of follow-ups (the coach's day; the Diary and its sheets; the shell, the primitives, Settings and the front door; the player's and the parent's side). CLAUDE.md: "Every surface on the white look".
- **22 November — art direction.** The next lesson is the one coloured card on a screen; the time leads a row; the log's areas are answers with a tick; a first-run empty tab is `EmptyHero`; Log a practice is the Drills tab's one `Button`. CLAUDE.md: "The next lesson is the one coloured card".
- **22–23 November — the club colours.** Twenty traditional colours at the business card's tone, a lead and a set for each sport, a role for each function, and a lighter half for every colour (pale, ledge, cloth) made in OKLCH by `scripts/club-palette.py`. Every key is filled: the one action deep, a function key in its cloth tone, a quiet key pale, a chosen answer the lead's cloth. CLAUDE.md: "The club colours, and every key filled with one".
- **23 November — the website.** Home, Product, Story and Partners under `website/`, built by plain Node into the same deploy at `/about/` (`npm run build` builds both). Its pictures are stills of the real app; its colours are the app's. **About Nosca** opens it from the sign-in page and from Settings.
- **23 November — the name.** An anglicised spelling of the Irish *nascadh*, linking — the founder's own account. The site says "From the Irish nascadh, linking."
- **23 November — the cards' address is a signpost.** The cards are printed with `nosca-pilot-2.netlify.app/install`. The app moved to `nosca.netlify.app`, a second Netlify site from the same repository; the first address sends every page on to the same page there, ahead of the bundle. The daily digest does nothing on the signpost, so nobody is sent two.
- **23 November — the splash.** The rings 1.3 times as wide and a quarter heavier, the animation untouched; on a sport's dark ground the light is the sport's cloth tone, which shows where the deep lead did not.
- **Who notices:** everybody, on every screen; anyone handed a card; anyone who taps About Nosca.
- **Not touched:** the tab bar and the raised plus, the feed, the walkthrough's shape, the splash's animation. No SQL.
