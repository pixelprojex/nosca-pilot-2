the Launch Book's "Decided" box into line with it (https://claude.ai/artifact/2sK2tixQYUDmK3zG5yqDrt).

same session and update the Launch Book.

  - Academy €140 a month: up to 200 players, 2 TB, about 200 lessons a month.
  - A year is ten months.
  - Pilot coaches are founding coaches and keep their first price for as long as they stay.
